### WG-174 — [FEAT] Show the live Twitch stream on the front page and release v1.9.0

On 2026-11-02 the owner asked to show his Twitch stream (channel `jacobleeyo`) on the wizardgang.ai front page while he is live. Add a `live` section between the hero and Capabilities that ships `hidden` in the static HTML, so visitors without JavaScript or while he is offline see nothing. Add `frame-src https://player.twitch.tv` to the CSP in `public/_headers` and leave every other directive alone. The section has a "Live on Twitch" heading and a link to `https://www.twitch.tv/jacobleeyo`, Spanish strings, existing tokens and a responsive 16:9 frame; the five named fragments and the navigation stay unchanged. Update the outbound-link and CSP contract tests, `SECURITY.md` and `docs/INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md`. Bump `package.json` and `package-lock.json` from 1.8.0 to 1.9.0. The live state can only be confirmed while the owner streams; the offline state is checked on the dev server.
